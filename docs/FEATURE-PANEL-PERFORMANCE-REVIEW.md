## Ownership bounds and caveats

The physical combined-process guard remains1GiB and raw working admission remains384MiB. Neither was raised; no GC, cache clearing, pressure trick, source reduction, image resize or pixel/format weakening was used by these leaves. Source-file admission retains the2GiB disk floor. Default asset limits remain20,000 entries; a million simultaneous images are outside that published limit. Million-stroke tests must respect the actual geometry/point/occurrence limits rather than implying unlimited live geometry.

Public OCR preserves40MiB encoded input and16,000,000 original pixels. At the policy bounds, UTF-16 base64 is111,848,112B, encoded bytes41,943,040B, filtered data at most134,217,728B, RGBA at most64,000,000B and slack33,554,432B. Maximum logical decode phases are:

| Phase | Live allocation formula | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| Inflate | source UTF-16 + encoded + private IDAT + filtered + slack |363,506,352B|
| RGBA placement | source UTF-16 + encoded + filtered + RGBA + slack |385,563,312B|
| Model | RGBA +96MiB model + at most1600²×48 detector/crop scratch + slack |321,097,728B|

IDAT is deterministically retired **before** RGBA allocation; the two decode formulas are distinct phases. Inflated scratch is protected even when RGBA allocation fails. Encoded/inflate scratch retires before model admission, with worker phase acknowledgement. Main queued jobs retain at most64 small metadata records, with a128MiB prospective encoded-byte admission limit; they do not carry full PNG strings. The initiating renderer still owns its original source, and the combined physical guard must include it. These policy formulas are not measurements of V8/WASM/allocator/GPU RSS.

After ready permission, Main must retain its transport claim until cleared source receipt, exact preload no-send/settled acknowledgement, or captured renderer PID actual exit. A deadline, window-close event or failed observer alone cannot release it. Lost acknowledgement while that PID remains alive can conservatively prolong this debt and delay the serialized queue; the source tests intentionally retain it. Shared-process renderer lifecycle and failure behavior require actual Electron evidence. OCR model retirement on completion favors bounded ownership; cold-start/reuse cost remains to be measured. Archive replacement-worker debt likewise remains until its actual previous consumer retires.
